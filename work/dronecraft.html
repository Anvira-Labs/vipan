DroneCraft Rides

A live video automation platform, built and run as sole engineer

DroneCraft Rides is a rafting and bungee operator in Shivpuri, India. It is the exclusive licence holder and sells the videos to all its partners. I am the sole architect and engineer of the platform that produces them.

Role
Sole architect and engineer, as Principal Software Architect (Consultant)
When
Sep 2026 – Present
Where
Client in Shivpuri, India
Team
Sole engineer
  • 2 formatsa landscape film and a portrait reel
  • Livein production, built and run by me

Challenge

The client's business is selling videos to all its partners, and the raw material is site camera and drone footage. That footage had to become finished, branded films through a repeatable automated process.

Approach

Ingest

The platform pulls site camera and drone footage from Google Drive to S3.

Auto-trim

Clips are trimmed automatically by motion and loudness.

Render

ffmpeg renders a landscape film and a portrait reel, adding branded intros, transitions, watermarks and per-camera audio mixing. Work runs through a SQLite job queue.

Results

  • Live in production.
  • Each run produces two formats: a landscape film and a portrait reel.
  • The client sells the videos to all its partners as the exclusive licence holder.
