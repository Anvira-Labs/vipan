Dresma

Serving GenAI imagery to 50,000+ brands on fewer than half the GPUs

I architected the platform behind Dresma, a GenAI e-commerce content SaaS serving the US, UK and Europe, and led its entire tech org. The company has since grown to US$2M ARR on the same infrastructure.

Role
Technical Architect & Lead Engineering Manager (Nov 2023 – Feb 2025); Senior Engineering Manager (Feb 2022 – Oct 2023)
When
Feb 2022 – Feb 2025
Where
HQ USA; engineering in Gurugram, India
Team
25–30 across AI/ML, DevOps, mobile, backend, frontend and QA, reporting to the CEO
  • 12 to 5GPU servers for the same output, about 58% fewer
  • 9M+images for 50,000+ brands, about 500,000 images a month
  • < 1 minfirst images on the premium tier, against a 2-hour SLA
  • US$2MARR the company has since reached on the same infrastructure

Challenge

Generating images with GenAI models takes GPUs, and GPU capacity was limited. DoMyShoot and the batch tools had to serve brands across three markets inside a 2-hour SLA, while the company also needed to reduce cost.

Approach

Multi-tenant GenAI pipelines

I architected cloud-native, multi-tenant pipelines behind DoMyShoot and the batch tools: Node.js on the backend, React on the frontend, Python for the models and Java for automation.

A tiered priority queue instead of more GPUs

Premium and standard queues ran across multi-cloud GPU capacity and cloud credits (AWS, GCP, Azure and private GPU partners). Premium users got first images in under a minute. Standard users got them in 10–15 minutes, well inside the 2-hour SLA.

In-house image models

We benchmarked open-source models and trained custom models on synthetic data.

Segmentation
SAM, U2-Net, BiRefNet
Detection, smart cropping
YOLO, Grounding DINO
Category classification
OpenAI CLIP
Generation
Stable Diffusion, SDXL
Listing text
Ollama self-hosted LLMs

Cost and organisation

Infrastructure streamlining, deployment automation and performance tuning cut hosting and processing costs by up to 30%. I also led the cost reduction and team resizing.

Results

  • Scaled DoMyShoot and batch tools to 50,000+ brands and 9M+ images, about 500,000 images a month.
  • Reduced GPU servers from 12 to 5 (about 58% fewer) for the same output.
  • First images in under 1 minute on the premium tier and 10–15 minutes on the standard tier, against a 2-hour SLA.
  • Cut hosting and processing costs by up to 30%.
  • The platform still powers Dresma, which has since grown to US$2M ARR on the same infrastructure, after my exit.
  • Company-reported Brand impact of up to 60% conversion uplift and 85% lower content costs. These are Dresma's figures, not ones I measured.
