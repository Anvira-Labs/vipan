SignCatch and Walmart KDP

Enabling the supply chain for Walmart India's Kirana Development Program

SignCatch is a B2B retail-tech company building POS and Retail Commerce-as-a-Service. As exclusive tech partner for Walmart India's Kirana Development Program (KDP), we connected neighbourhood kirana stores to Walmart's replenishment centres.

Role
Senior Software Architect, reporting to the CEO
When
Sep 2019 – May 2021
Where
Incorporated in California, USA; engineering in New Delhi
Team
25 across backend, frontend, mobile, DevOps, QA and AI
  • 500kirana stores onboarded in beta and connected to Walmart replenishment centres
  • 700+retail locations: about 200 merchants plus 500 KDP stores
  • 100,000+organic downloads of a hyperlocal Android app in 6 months
  • ISO 27001certification achieved

Challenge

Walmart's KDP needed a technology partner to connect independent kirana stores to its replenishment centres. The same platform also had to serve restaurants, tuck shops, general stores and multi-store chains, and it was still running on legacy systems.

Approach

A multi-tenant, multi-vertical POS

One POS served several retail verticals and gave each store its own online storefront. Omnichannel POS-Pro covered billing, inventory, loyalty and CRM.

Multi-cloud for a major partner

I led the full integration and migration: AWS as the core, Azure for Walmart KDP.

Platform and security

I migrated legacy systems to microservices and aligned infrastructure and processes to security standards to achieve ISO 27001.

Forecasting

With the AI team, I built an internal demand-forecasting tool that was shared with Walmart.

Organisation

I also led cost reduction and team resizing.

Results

  • Enabled the supply chain for Walmart India's KDP as exclusive tech partner, onboarding 500 kirana stores in beta.
  • Grew the platform to 700+ retail locations (about 200 merchants plus 500 KDP stores).
  • Achieved ISO 27001 certification.
  • Ran multi-cloud with AWS as the core and Azure for Walmart KDP.
  • Grew a hyperlocal Android app to 100,000+ organic downloads in 6 months.
